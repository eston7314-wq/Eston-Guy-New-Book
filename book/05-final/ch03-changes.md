2. **Parables are named with capitals, "parable" in lower case:** the Good Samaritan, the Sower, the Prodigal Son, the Lost Son; "the parable of the Sower". People in a parable are lower case (the father, the elder brother, the younger son, a priest, a Levite, the lawyer); "the Samaritan" is capitalised because it names a people.

Music stays lower case: "gospel music", "gospel hymns" (ch02). A New Testament letter is described in lower case in narration ("the first letter to the Corinthians") and given in full in references ("1 Corinthians 15:55, 57").
4. **Kingdom of God** is capitalised in narration, and so is its short form ("the message of the Kingdom"). Inside KJV quotations the KJV's lower case stands ("the kingdom of God", "the kingdom of heaven"). Source: the thesis's narration (1738-1740) and the author's KJV decision (2026-11-13).
