## Title collision

- "Thy Faith Hath Made Thee Whole" is already the title of Carol Jackson Robinson, *Thy Faith Hath
  Made Thee Whole: The Integrity Years (1946-1956)*, Arouca Press, 2021 (hardback and paperback).
  Titles cannot be copyrighted, but it affects search results. The phrase is also the title of several
  older hymns (hymnary.org).
- No book titled "A Fortress, Not a Prison" was found (search 2026-11-07).

## Category observations

- The search results for Black church and mental health are dominated by US titles and small self-published
  books. No UK lay-reader book on faith and mental health for people of African heritage was found.
- None of the comparables covers the UK, the USA and Canada together.
