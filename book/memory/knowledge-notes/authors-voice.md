## Observed in the thesis front matter and preface sources (chapter-writer, 2026-11-10; source thesis-2025.md 66-121, 506-515, 4921-4923)

- Formal, courteous openings: "I am delighted to acknowledge", "I acknowledge also", "Moreover", "Similarly", "Above all". Keep one or two per chapter for voice; strip the rest in plain-language passages.

- Warm evaluative words about people: "fantastic multi-ethnic group", "valuable support". Keep "fantastic"; "valuable" is on the humanizer list, so rephrase.

- "In some unique way" is his idiom for a felt connection he does not explain (95). Keep it.
- Ends sections by thanking God (121). One such line per chapter at most, where the source has it.
- Uses "Public school" for state schools (106-107); UK readers will misread it. Ask him before restoring.
