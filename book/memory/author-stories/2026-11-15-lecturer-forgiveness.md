# Author story: the lecturer who marked him down, and forgiveness (given 2026-11-15)

> I believe that one of my lecturers at the University of London was unfair in marking me down in some of my essays in one area of my study of Contemporary Religions. When she gave me a grade B+ for a resubmitted essay, I knew then that my belief was justified. A fellow student was given a grade A* for an essay that I deliberately copied, but changed the beginnings  of some  sentences and adverbs, while presenting the same content and style of writing. I was given a B+ for the resubmitted essay.
>
> In order to disprove my suspicion, I asked my Philosophy lecturer for an assessment of the quality of my essays, and was told that I was among the top 5 undergraduates in a group of seventeen. I love to write essays because I have always researched the topics, yet no matter how hard I tried, that Religious Studies lecturer marked me down.
>
> The feedback from my Philosophy lecturer made me more determined to continue working smarter, not only to succeed, but also to prove the  unfairness of  lecturer.  At that time, I could not forget my feeling of being treated unfairly, but I asked God to help me forgive.
>
> I felt that  my silent prayers were answered when at the end of my third year, only one other student and I, from the group of twenty-five studying  Contemporary Religion achieved the required grades to go on to the final year and gained our Bachelor in Education Degree with Honours. The others achieved only their Certificates in Education.
>
> Surprisingly, the lecturer who had marked me down over the years, was the first to write to me during the Summer holidays to congratulate me on my success.
