> As their  Head of Year, I had prayed for the success of my group of students whop achieved the highest Standard Attainment Tests (SATs) results in the history of Gladesmore Community School in North London, England in 2002. I thanked God for enabling those students to benefit from their hard work and dedication and that of the teaching staff, their voluntary Academic Mentors, the school's ancillary staff, the support of their parents and the leadership of our new Headteacher, Mr T. Hartney. Furthermore, the class of 2004, continued to work smartly and benefited also from a new Academic and Pastoral programme that was implemented by the school. I held the roles of Head of Pastoral Care, Head of Year 11, Co-ordinator of the Gifted and Talented Students' Programme and Assistant Headteacher while teaching Religious Education. My faith helped me to maintain a belief that God helps those who were willing to help themselves and others. In their Year book, the Headteacher wrote "You have worked with the school and have learnt that hard work brings rewards of progress and results." The Class of 2004 went on to achieve the best GCSE results in the history of the school, up to 2004. Like others, I believed in God's rewards.
