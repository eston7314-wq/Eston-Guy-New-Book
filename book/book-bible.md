# Book Bible

The running memory of the manuscript. Every writing or editing agent reads this before touching
a chapter and appends to it after. If it is not in here, a later chapter will contradict it or
repeat it.

## Voice and style rules

- Primary tone: warm and pastoral (author decision 2026-11-07, book.config.md)
- Sentence rhythm: mostly short. One long sentence per paragraph at most.
- Banned phrases: "in today's fast-paced world", "delve", "it's important to note",
  "game-changer", "unlock", "navigate the landscape", "at the end of the day",
  "in this chapter we will", any sentence that begins "Imagine a world where".
- Banned structures: three-item lists in every paragraph; rhetorical question openers
  more than once per chapter; a summary that restates the headings; a conclusion that recaps
  chapter titles.
- **Humanizer rules apply to every chapter** (author, 2026-11-10): read `.claude/skills/humanizer/SKILL.md` before writing or editing. No not-X-but-Y contrasts, one-line closers, staged openers, forced triads, dashes everywhere, inflated claims, sales language, stock AI words, bold labels or filler.
- The author writes as one of the community (author, 2026-11-10): "we", "us" and "our ancestors" for people of African heritage are allowed and welcome.
- Reader is addressed as "you". Author is "I" (or "we" if config says so).

### Consistency decisions (copy editor appends; later chapters follow)

- (strategist, 2026-11-07, pending author) Scripture is quoted in the KJV/AKJV wording the thesis uses until the author chooses a translation. "The writer to the Hebrews", not "Paul", for Hebrews. Use "African heritage" as the umbrella term; use "Black Church" only for the institution.
- (strategist, 2026-11-07) Suicide passages follow the Samaritans media guidelines: no methods, and a help line nearby.
- (copy-editor, 2026-11-11, ch00; full list in `book/style-sheet.md`) **Spelling:** British -ise (config rule 4); proper names keep their own spelling ("World Health Organization", "Centers for Disease Control and Prevention"), and our paraphrase of them is British.
- (copy-editor, 2026-11-11) **Serial comma:** none (config rule 4). Where a list is ambiguous without it, recast the sentence; never restore the comma. A comma before "and" that joins clauses, or two items, is allowed.
- (copy-editor, 2026-11-11) **Scripture references:** "quotation" (Luke 8:48, KJV): book in full, chapter:verse, translation after a comma, one pair of parentheses. Every quotation carries its translation until the author chooses one. Lowercase "scripture" and "the psalms" (individual psalms); capitalise the Bible, Bible study, the New Testament, the Eucharist, Psalms (the book).
- (copy-editor, 2026-11-11) **Chapter references:** "chapter 4" (lowercase mid-sentence, no leading zero; never "chapter 04"); Part One to Part Four; Appendix A and B; "the preface", "the afterword".
- (copy-editor, 2026-11-11) **Numbers:** words for one to ten in prose; figures for 11 and up and for every count or survey figure in a data sentence ("20 in the UK ... 30 women and 20 men", never "thirty women"); "N of the 50"; spell out at the start of a sentence; "fifty" in words for the group in narrative ("what fifty people told me"). Dates: 21 April 2025. Phone numbers as each service writes them.
- (copy-editor, 2026-11-11) **Quotation marks:** double, with single inside; straight in the files and curled at typesetting; British logical punctuation; the citation after the closing mark.
- (copy-editor, 2026-11-11) **Also:** UK, USA (noun), US (adjective), CDC, WHO, PhD, GCSE, KJV without stops. Initials spaced with stops (F. W. G. Perkins). Specific posts and named courses capitalised (Head of Year 11, Contemporary Religions); generic roles lowercase. "mental-health" before a noun, "mental health" as a noun; "well-being"; African American / British / Canadian with no hyphen; Afro-Caribbean and mixed-race hyphenated. H1 in title case, H2/H3 in sentence case.
- (copy-editor, 2026-11-11, ch00 author revisions) **Course name:** "Contemporary Religions" (plural), the same subject at the University of London and at school (author, 2026-11-10). Never "Contemporary Religion".
- (copy-editor, 2026-11-11) **Institutions:** full name, capitalised: "the University of London", "Selinus University of Science and Literature" ("the" lowercase mid-sentence). Generic "university" and "school" are lowercase (Hart's Rules, proper names).
- (copy-editor, 2026-11-11) **Author statements stay in his words:** where the author supplies his own sentence in place of a story flag, keep his wording and punctuation. Add only a minimal lead-in and fix capitalisation, then mark it `<!-- author-confirmed DATE -->` (ch00 precedent; config rules 2, 5 and 9).
