## Strongest counter-evidence (for the developmental editor)

The chronic-illness link runs both ways, and the sources put more weight on depression as a risk
for physical illness than on physical illness shaping behaviour. NICE CG91 also notes overlapping
symptoms (such as fatigue) that make depression harder to recognise in people with chronic illness.
The preface's general claim holds; its first clause, as written, has the direction the sources
least support.
