## 1. ILLUSTRATIVE (hypothetical): the verse that steadied and the words that wounded

Placed in the preface, section "Faith gives many people a home, and a home can also hurt",
paragraph 2. Introduced in the text with "Think of". It expands the draft's existing general
sentence (a verse that steadies a frightened mind; the same verse used carelessly by someone in
authority) into one scene. No statistic, verse, name or place is attached.

> Think of a woman lying awake the night before a hospital appointment. She reads a verse she has
> known since childhood, and her fear eases enough for her to sleep. Months later she tells a
> leader at her church how low she has been feeling, and is told that if her faith were stronger
> she would not feel this way. She goes home believing that her illness is her own fault. Both
> moments came from the same faith, and the second came from someone in authority using it
> carelessly.

Owner notes: the guilt theme belongs to ch07 ("mental illness is not moral failure"); ch07 should
not reuse this woman or the hospital-night scene, and may call back to it only by reference.
