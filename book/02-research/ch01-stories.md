## Real scenes shaped in this pass (for the record; NOT illustrative)

Both texts are public domain and were opened in full by the researcher (ch01-evidence.md section 6
and 7) and re-read in the same GITenberg files by the story-injector on 2026-11-11. The scenes are
paraphrased; the only words in quotation marks are passages the pack lists as verified.

1. **REAL: Mary Prince, the Christmas prayer meeting at Winthorps, Antigua.** Prince 1831, *The
   History of Mary Prince*, Project Gutenberg #17851. An enslaved woman takes Mary to a Methodist
   prayer meeting at her husband's house; the husband, Henry, a Black driver ("ranger" in her text),
   confesses his cruelty to the enslaved, says his master's orders compelled him, and that a driver
   must sometimes beat his own wife or sister if ordered; he asks them all to forgive him and prays
   God to forgive him; Mary cries the whole night, too ashamed to speak. The meeting "led my spirit
   to the Moravian church". Placed in section 4 ("The wound is carried..."), proving "spiritual
   alienation, guilt and mental anguish". Section 3 introduces the meeting and adds that she went to
   the Moravian teachers secretly, running round when she took the children's lunch to school.

2. **REAL: Frederick Douglass, Thomas Auld's conversion.** Douglass 1845, *Narrative*, ch. IX,
   Project Gutenberg #23. His master was converted at a Methodist camp meeting in August 1832 and,
   in Douglass's judgement, became worse, because he now found "religious sanction" for his
   cruelty. Placed in section 1 before the existing whipping scene, proving that scripture was
   used to justify cruelty.
