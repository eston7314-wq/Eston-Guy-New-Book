Who is at risk, in the thesis's own direction: rates among people of African heritage lower overall than in the wider population in the UK and Canada, highest among young Black men in all three countries (3143-3156, 3236-3244). The church as a bridge: spiritual care with referral to medical or psychological services (3282-3289), leaders who notice withdrawal, hopelessness and talk of death (3277-3280), and a church that knows its trust is a responsibility (3252-3255). On domestic abuse: Ephesians 5:22, Colossians 3:18 and "turn the other cheek" used to keep victims in danger (3321-3338); the CPS definition (3346-3350); a survivor told "God hates divorce" by one church and helped to leave by another (3462-3480); women who disclose in prayer groups or to a friend at church (3499-3504); leaders "keen to be appropriately trained" (3441-3445); churches working with statutory agencies (3566-3577).
