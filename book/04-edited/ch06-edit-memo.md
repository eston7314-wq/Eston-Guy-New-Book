## What was wrong, and what I did about it

The draft is careful and honest. It opens on a real case told exactly as pack 1.3 allows. It quotes scripture only from recorded KJV readings, frames every benefit as belief, and gives the counter-evidence its due ("Is Christ divided?", "A first door is only as good as where it leads", "Comfort had not healed that trauma", the bridge). What was wrong was in five places.

1. **The shift was named but never argued.** Blueprint v2 asks the chapter to move the reader from "Church is where I go on Sunday" / "My troubles are private" to "My church can be a family that carries me, and I can help carry others." The draft named both starting points only inside its final sentence ("If you have thought of church as the place you go on a Sunday, or of your troubles as nobody else's business, I hope you will see it another way"), and answered neither. The sceptical reader this chapter most needs is the private one: someone who keeps their troubles to themselves, often because a church has let them down. A hope is not an answer to that reader. The chapter already holds the answer. Social isolation is on its own list of risk factors (thesis 1216), and Galatians 6:2 needs a burden that someone knows about. I gave the shift its own paragraph (D15). It answers the Sunday reader from what the chapter has shown, the services and "all week long", and the private reader from the risk list and Galatians. It grants the reader's caution in one clause, and the bridge's next sentence, "Not every family is a safe one, and not every church is either", grants the danger in full and hands it to ch07. This is the edit that matters most. It adds no fact, name, number or quoted span.
2. **Six sections for five arguments, and one heading that did not cover its section.** Blueprint argument 2 is one argument: the church as a family house, with Acts 2 and Galatians 6:2 as its charter. The draft split it into two H2s, and the break fell between Mark 2's four carriers and the family house, which is where the argument turns. I merged them under "The first believers carried one another, and many Black churches still do, all week long" (D5), with a sentence that makes the turn follow from the scene (D8). The crisis heading, "When disaster comes, the house opens its doors", did not cover the section's last paragraph: the Racial Justice Advocacy Forum and St Paul's are about racial injustice and policing, not disaster. I widened the heading (D11) rather than move the paragraph. Moving it is the better fit for the argument, but the trade-off is set out under "Considered and not done".
3. **The argument broke in two paragraphs.** In the protective-factors section, the WHO sentence sat between "it does not make anyone immune" and the sentence that proves it (D4). In the first-door section, the resilience-and-belonging paragraph sat between the first-door evidence (Taylor, flags 85 and 80) and Eli, so the thread from "the first place many people go" to "which of those two answers you meet" to "A first door is only as good as where it leads" was cut in half (D10). Both are now in order. Nothing is dropped, and the flags move with their sentences.
4. **The landing defined its images by rule.** After Eston's line, "an anchor, a shield, a sanctuary", the writer gave three parallel sentences of the form "An X does Y, as Z". Each sentence ties the word to the chapter, which is worth keeping. But three matching definitions after a three-word line is the humanizer's triad at paragraph scale, and it spends the chapter's best line on a dictionary. One sentence now keeps each word's place in the chapter (D14). The definitions are on the cut list (row 23).
5. **Repeats, signposts and one overstatement.** The opening's second sentence retold the paragraph before it ("a steady flow" twice in two paragraphs) just as the chapter's question was being asked, and "before anyone was asked to come" says more than thesis 1400-1404 (D1; row 22). Other changes: two "Chapter 1 told how" openings (D2); "a supportive family environment and a supportive faith environment" said twice in two sentences (D3); "In my thesis" used nine times (D6, one varied); a repeated Galatians span without its reference (D7); "Its work today belongs here." and "The churches are not all alike." (D8, D9). "It stands beside the other help that people need, and never takes its place" was written as a description of what church care does. As a description it is false, and ch07 exists because it is false. It is now the purpose Eston believes in (D12). The three-country section did not say why the USA was missing from "all three countries" (D13).
