## What was wrong, and what I did about it

1. **Section 2 told the prevention story out of order.** The draft said "By 2008 there was still no established suicide prevention programme for African American youth" and then, in the next sentence, "More Christians ... began to focus on prevention, leading ... to a new awakening in Black churches". A reader takes the second as contradicting the first. In the thesis the absence of programmes opens the CDC paragraph (3200, "In the absence of suicide prevention programmes ..."), so the sentence now opens that paragraph (A2). The Canadian sentence about a multi-agency approach ("the rates show the continued need ...") sat between chapter 7's "not merely as spiritual failings" and the trust placed in rural churches. It has nothing to do with either. It now follows the Canadian rates, as in the thesis (3244-3247), as "These rates, I wrote, show ..." (A1). The next paragraph now says why it follows: "The Black Church in the USA fits that description: ..." picks up the CDC's three tests (D7).
2. **Section 3 answered the objection before raising it.** The draft read Ephesians 5:21-29 whole and only then quoted Mark 10:9 and Ephesians 5:23 as "other verses quoted against leaving". A traditional reader meets "the husband is the head of the wife" after the answer to it. The counter-texts paragraph now comes first. It ends on the thesis's reply ("the harm comes from the way religious teachings are applied"), and the reading of the passage whole then shows what that means, with 5:25 straight after 5:23's "head" (A3). "The same is true of forgiveness" became "Forgiveness has been misused in the same way", because it now follows a reading, not a claim. I also split the long forgiveness paragraph before "Jesus said", so the Matthew 5:38-39 reading stands as the answer, as the Ephesians reading does.
3. **The shift line was said twice.** The counter-texts paragraph ended "and I do not believe any of these verses requires a person to stay where they are being hurt." The landing says "I do not believe any scripture requires anyone to stay in danger." The first was the writer's clause, and it spent the landing three sections early. Cut (D4). The belief now arrives once, at the end, after the case has shown it.
4. **One sentence promised more than the case delivers.** "The story in its last section shows forgiveness asking no one to stay in harm's way" is not true of the story: Brittney's account never mentions forgiveness. A sceptical reader who turns to the case would find the promise unpaid. The Matthew 5:38-39 reading carries the forgiveness half of the ch03 and ch08 promise. The sentence now claims only what the case shows: "the story in the last section shows a church telling a woman she did not have to stay." (D5).
5. **The Catechism paragraph did not meet its strongest objection.** The pack (section 8, point 2) warns that "can diminish the responsibility" assumes the act is still held to be wrong, so the book must not imply that the Church has stopped teaching this. The writer's "The Church now speaks of diminished responsibility, and of compassion." restated the sentence after it ("with compassion") and did not meet the point. It is replaced with "As I read it, that sentence lessens the blame on the person without excusing the act." (D3). This is a reading of the quoted words only, in his "As I read it" form, and says nothing about the rest of the Catechism. Pack question 9c is still his to answer. If he answers it, this sentence may change.
6. **Repeats and frames.** The John 10:10 gloss restated the verse (D2). The thesis's "thinking of prevention became uppermost" was used twice, once at the end of section 1 and again where section 2's prevention story begins. The section 1 use is cut (D6, cut-list row 44). "And have let it go on for a long time" repeated "tolerated for too long" from the sentence before (D9, row 45). I removed attribution frames only where the same paragraph already carried one, and never inside a flagged sentence (D1). The closer quoted the Ephesians span twice and asked for 5:21-25 to be read twice (D10).
