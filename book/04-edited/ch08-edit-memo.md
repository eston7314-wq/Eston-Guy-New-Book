## What was wrong, and what I did about it

The draft is careful. Every belief is framed as a belief, every clinical sentence carries a clinician note, and spent lines are called back, not restated. The story pass gave sections 1 and 2 a scene each. The problems were in five places.

1. **One callback said the opposite of what chapter 1 says.** Right after the flagged "PTSD is widespread ... due to the legacy of slavery", the draft read "Chapter 1 also suggested that the harm to our mental health shows most in how long suffering lasts ...". The word "also" made chapter 1 a second witness for the sentence it actually cautions against. "Shows most in" also paraphrased chapter 1's contrast, "shows less in how often we fall ill than in how long suffering lasts", and dropped its first half, the half that matters here. The passage now says chapter 1 "was more careful about how often illness comes among us" and gives chapter 1's own words. The section's weight then moves, as the draft intended, to trauma left untreated (D7). His sentence and its flag are unchanged, so the tension is named in the prose for the reader and left for him to settle (pack question 2). This is the edit that matters most.
2. **The opening said its framing twice.** The frame paragraph spent three sentences on things the preface and chapter 1 have already told the reader: faith's influence on how we think and cope (ch00 29-31), being "honest about both sides" (ch00 33) and the history of trauma and oppression (ch01). The paragraph only reached the material ch08 uses (the strands, Paul's letters, the leitmotif) after those three sentences. They are cut and on the cut list, rows 15-17 (D3). The opening also named the four conditions in its first paragraph and then introduced them again two paragraphs later as "the conditions I called most prevalent". The names now come once, where the thesis introduces them (D1). The opening paragraph now ends on chapter 7's own words. Chapter 7 had also been called back in two paragraphs in a row (D2).
3. **The addiction section's summary pointed at the wrong paragraphs.** "These passages show ... the New Testament gives ... ample guidance on the dangers of substance abuse" stood at the end of the London and Toronto programmes paragraph. That put three paragraphs about churches between "these passages" and the verses it sums up. Galatians 6:1 then turns to the community and the section moves on to the churches (D8). No flag moved.

The section then closed on "each of the four sections above has come back to it", which pointed at the shift without stating it. The first repeat is cut. The last sentence now states the blueprint's shift (D9).
5. **Repeats and staging in smaller places.** "That is where the naming begins." announced section 1 without adding to it (D4). "James is asking for patience" was followed by "the patience is given room" (D5). "the honest answer is this:" staged the answer before giving it (D6).
