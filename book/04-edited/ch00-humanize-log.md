## Deliberately left alone (checked, judged deliberate or the author's)

- **"New Testament Christianity plays a very significant role in the lives of many people of African heritage."** This looks like §13 ("plays a key role"), but it is the author's own sentence word for word (thesis 506). It is his register, and it opens his own background chapter. Kept for voice.
- **Triads in the home section** ("redemption, hope and community"; "teachings, parables and miracles"; "think, feel and cope"). The first two are from the source (thesis 51-53, 507-509), and the dev edit already merged them into one sentence. In "think, feel and cope" each item does its own job: the story that follows is about fear (feel) and sleeping or not (cope). Not forced. Kept.
- **The two "Think of" openers** (the woman before her hospital appointment; the reflection). The dev edit flagged these for this pass. They do different jobs. The first marks the woman as imagined, which the story brief requires (ILLUSTRATIVE). The second is an instruction to the reader in the reflection. Neither is a staged run-up. Kept.
- **"I will be honest about both sides."** This is not §4 staged candour. It is a promise recorded in the Book Bible (harm paid off in 07-09), and the next sentence delivers on it. Kept.
- **"I will not ask you to choose between your faith and your doctor."** This is not §5 arguing with no one. The reader really does hold this fear (it is the book's core transformation), and it is a Book Bible promise. Kept.
- **"Fifty people cannot speak for everyone of African heritage, and I will not pretend they do."** This corrects a belief a reader might really form from the numbers, and hard rule 10 requires the limit. Kept.
- **"A word is defined by how people use it in daily life, more than by any logical structure that lies beneath it."** This has the form of §1 (X rather than Y), but both halves carry information. They state Wittgenstein's actual contrast. Kept.
- **"So before we go further, here is how I use the words this book leans on most."** This is a mild announcement, but it ties the Wittgenstein point (meaning is use) to the author's own use. It works as an ordinary book transition and is not a chat run-up. Kept.
- **"Two of the overall numbers belong here at the start."** "Overall" carries the consent framing (hard rule 10), so the sentence earns its place. Kept. The two survey sentences after it are word for word and untouched.
- **"Faith is untidier than any three words."** This is a short sentence, but not a one-line paragraph. It adds a claim (the lens has limits), and the dev memo uses it against the counter-evidence. Kept.
- **The parts paragraph** (four sentences opening "Part One / Two / Three / Four"). This is a book map, so the repetition is the structure and not §7. Kept.
- **The 2004 school paragraph** (supervised / managed / held). The dev memo suggested folding "I also managed..." into the sentence before, *once the story is in*. The story is not in. The three sentences do not share an opening, so §7 does not apply, and folding them would make one long, stacked sentence of the kind the voice note says to split.
