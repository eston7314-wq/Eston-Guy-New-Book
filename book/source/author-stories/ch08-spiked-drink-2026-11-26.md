> A young undergraduate in London recalled that he had unwittingly taken home a small bottle of coca cola that was given to him as surplus at a friend's birthday party that he had  attended on a Saturday evening, in the district where he lived before going off to university.
>
> After drinking half of the Coca Cola, he felt, dizzy and unsteady, but when he tried to walk to his bed, he fell and knocked over a heavy table lamp. His parents were startled by the terrific thump coming from their son's room above. Mum and Dad rushed upstairs to their son's bedroom to investigate.
>
> They found their son on the floor. He had vomited, and looked drunk, but there was no smell of alcohol. He tried to explain how he felt, but his speech was slurred and he reported that he did not understand  what was happening to him.  Mum and Dad looked around the room to see if they could find anything that may help to show why the young man was in that condition. As the boy's condition worsened, Dad called 999, and agreed to take him to the Accident and Emergency Department of their nearest hospital to avoid an indefinite wait for an ambulance.  As they travelled, mum, an ardent christian,  called her sister who immediately began to pray for her nephew's recovery. The sisters did not want to speculate, but both feared that the young man may have had some serious health condition.
>
> The young man was rushed into a cubicle where he was examined immediately. The nurse assisted the doctor who  took information from the parents as part of their investigation. The patient was hallucinating, and when he  complained of  blurred vision, the doctor asked the parents whether he was known to take illegal substances. His parents said that they were shocked at that line of questioning, because their son was known to be responsible, and fully aware of the dangers of substance misuse. He had expressed his disgust about young people who were "killing themselves and destroying their future by taking drugs..." However, Dad recalled seeing his son holding a bottle of coca cola when he returned home from the party.
>
> The blood test revealed that the young man had traces of an illegal substance in his blood. He was given appropriate treatment to prevent further adverse effect of the drug, and a test of the remaining content of the bottle confirmed the doctor's finding.
>
> The young man was discharged from hospital the following afternoon. He was questioned by hospital personnel to determine who may have spiked his drink, but his parents decided not to report the matter to the police. They were very disappointed with their son's lack of "diligence", but believed that "God should be the judge." They believed that their son had learned a painful lesson that would guide him in choosing friends and in deciding who to trust.
