After spending five years at Alexandra Park School where I attained the position of Head of Religious Studies, and a Masters Degree from the University of London (part-time), I resigned to take my family to Trinidad and Tobago.

Our main aim was to give Nigel and Duane the type of Primary Education that Myrtle and I had.  Secondly, I wanted to research for my PhD in Religions at the University of the West Indies, St. Augustine Campus, Trinidad.

Unfortunately, circumstances changed and I took a temporary position as Lecturer in Education at the Catholic Women Teachers' College before moving to the permanent position of Training Officer 11 (Senior Training Officer) at the Ministry of Health, Port-of-Spain. Myrtle worked as a Staff Nurse in the same Ministry, while Nigel and Duane attended Rosary Boys Primary School. Nigel then went on to St. Mary's College, and Duane studied at Tranquility Boys Secondary school in Port-of-Spain.

While at the Ministry, a close relative who had lost her husband in a tragic vehicular accident, developed a serious medical condition that did not respond effectively to her doctor's treatment. As a practising christian, she prayed to God for help and relief of severe pain. When she explained her condition to me, I thought immediately of the many doctors and other health professionals for whom I had  secured post-graduate and post-registration scholarships or training to help  provide a better National Health Service. I obtained  my relative's permission to discuss her condition with one of our Consultants who took up the case immediately, After the necessary examinations and preparation, my relative received the necessary surgery that brought relief and an end to her suffering that enveloped her life for about two years. Her family and I thanked God for enabling the care and attention that brought physical and mental healing to the family. We reaffirmed our faith in God's power top help those in need.
