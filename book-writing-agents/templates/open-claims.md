plus items the researcher could not confirm during evidence gathering. The author decides each
one (config rules 1 and 9). When the author confirms an item, replace the flag in the chapter
with `<!-- author-confirmed YYYY-MM-DD -->` and mark the row "resolved" here. Append; never delete.
