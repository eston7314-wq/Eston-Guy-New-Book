# Stage 6 Playbook: Marketing

> **Agent**: Book Marketer | **Pre-condition**: introduction and conclusion final; author has confirmed the credibility lines in the config

## Objective
Titles, cover brief, blurb, Amazon description, bio, and table of contents that the manuscript can support.

## Inputs
config, Book Bible (promises, terms, locked title), blueprint, final introduction and conclusion, strategist's comparable-title note.

## Outputs
`book/06-marketing/` six files; Book Bible: locked title, subtitle, taglines.

## Quality gate
- [ ] 20 titles, 5 search-optimised, shortlist of 5 for reader testing
- [ ] Three cover directions with hex palettes and generation prompts
- [ ] Blurb under 100 words; description in KDP-allowed HTML; 7 keywords under 50 characters; 3 categories with the winnable one named
- [ ] Bio built only from confirmed credibility
- [ ] Zero claims the manuscript cannot support

## Handoff
Author locks the title and subtitle; orchestrator records it; stage 7 follows.
