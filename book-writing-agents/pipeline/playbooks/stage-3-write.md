# Stage 3 Playbook: Write (draft or consolidate) and Story Pass

> **Agents**: Chapter Writer, then Story Injector | **Sequential**: always

## Objective
One chapter, in the author's voice, inside the word range, with every fact traceable and every cut listed.

## Pre-conditions
- [ ] Evidence pack exists
- [ ] Previous chapter's latest file exists (except for 00)
- [ ] Orchestrator has decided draft versus consolidate from the config's source row

## Inputs
config, memory, Book Bible, blueprint block, evidence pack, previous chapter, source passages (consolidate).

## Outputs
`book/03-drafts/chNN-slug.md`; `chNN-cut-list.md` (consolidate); after the story pass, the revised draft and `chNN-slug.pre-story.md`; Book Bible rows.

## The consolidate rule
The author has already written this chapter, several times over. The writer finds the one chapter inside the material. Cut freely: duplicates, chat commentary, padding. Everything else left out is a cut-list row. The author decides.

## Quality gate (writer)
- [ ] Word count inside the config range
- [ ] Zero unflagged facts; zero invented author stories
- [ ] Banned phrases and structures absent
- [ ] Editor notes list the spine used and three weakest spots

## Quality gate (story pass)
- [ ] Hook within three sentences
- [ ] Each added illustration labelled real (cited) or composite (signalled in prose)
- [ ] Growth at or under 15 percent; budget held where already met
- [ ] `[AUTHOR STORY NEEDED]` slots untouched

## Handoff
Chapter card: spine used, what was cut and why, illustrations added and their status, what the developmental editor should look at first.
