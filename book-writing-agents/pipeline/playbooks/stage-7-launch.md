# Stage 7 Playbook: Launch

> **Agent**: Launch Strategist | **Pre-condition**: marketing files exist; launch date set in the config

## Objective
A dated, checklist-form path from manuscript to live listing to a realistic first 30 days.

## Inputs
config, marketing files, Book Bible, `knowledge-notes/kdp-rules.md`.

## Outputs
`book/07-launch/launch-plan.md`, `arc-outreach.md`, `social-campaign.md`, `email-sequence.md`; `kdp-rules.md` filled with dated, sourced rules.

## Quality gate
- [ ] Every platform fact (royalty tiers, price bands, category limits, Select terms, review policy, AI disclosure) checked and dated
- [ ] Realistic daily sales for rank one in the chosen category stated
- [ ] Timeline day -30 to +30 dated from the config
- [ ] Outreach templates inside review policy

## Handoff
Author decisions: launch date, KDP Select, free window, price. Then build, convert, upload.
