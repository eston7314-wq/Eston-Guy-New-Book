# Stage 1 Playbook: Blueprint

> **Agent**: Book Strategist (Mode B, or enrichment when the author's structure is locked) | **Gate keeper**: orchestrator

## Objective
A locked, chapter-by-chapter blueprint every later agent can execute against, and a config with no placeholders in topic, reader, or tone.

## Pre-conditions
- [ ] A chosen concept (stage 0) or the author's own outline in `book/01-outline/author-guidelines.md`
- [ ] If the author's structure exists, the orchestrator has told the strategist not to reorder or rename

## Inputs
config, profile, Book Bible, source map and source drafts if any, author guidelines.

## Outputs
`book/01-outline/blueprint.md`; config rows filled; Book Bible promises and frameworks tables; chapter status table titles with Outline = done.

## Quality gate
- [ ] Every chapter block has arguments, shift, story slot, hook, budget, promises
- [ ] Structural risks section names overlaps, sag, and image ownership
- [ ] Budgets sum inside the config's book length range
- [ ] Config placeholders remaining are listed as author-only decisions

## Handoff
Orchestrator reports the three biggest structural risks to the author, records decisions, and moves to `chapter 00`.
