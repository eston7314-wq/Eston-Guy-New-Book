# Idea generator (merged from 5 source prompts)

Act as an experienced publishing strategist and market researcher with a track record of
bestselling titles. My niche is [YOUR NICHE]. Generate 10 book ideas that solve real problems
for a clearly defined audience. For each idea include:

- A working title paired with a persuasive subtitle
- Target reader (age, profession, interests, pain points)
- The core problem the book solves and why people would buy it now
- Comparable bestselling books
- A differentiated positioning statement explaining how this stands apart from competing titles
- Relevant trends or cultural shifts that align with current demand
- A realistic estimate of the addressable market
- Compelling reasons readers would confidently invest $20-$30 in it
- Estimated commercial potential (High/Medium/Low) with reasoning

Rank the ideas from strongest to weakest and recommend the best one.

Sources merged: "Find a Book People Actually Want to Read", "Idea Generator" (x3),
"High-Impact Book Idea Architect".
