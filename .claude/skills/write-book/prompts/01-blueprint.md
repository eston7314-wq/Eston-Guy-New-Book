# Book blueprint (merged from 6 source prompts)

You are a bestselling non-fiction book strategist and professional book architect. I want to
write a book about [TOPIC] for [TARGET AUDIENCE], titled [BOOK TITLE]. Give me:

- A compelling title + subtitle
- The core transformation the reader gets and a compelling promise
- The positioning angle that makes this different from every other book on Amazon
- An introduction brief with an attention-grabbing hook for Chapter One
- 10-15 thoughtfully sequenced chapters. For each: a clear, benefit-driven title; 3-5 essential
  concepts or arguments; 3-7 actionable lessons; where case studies, stories, or examples belong;
  a projected word count (1,500-3,000); the intended transformation or insight by the chapter's
  end; an emotional hook that makes the reader want the next chapter; a logical transition into
  the following chapter
- Key takeaways for every chapter
- A conclusion with next steps for readers and a satisfying close that reinforces the promise

Ensure the structure builds knowledge progressively and avoids repetition.

Sources merged: "The Book Blueprint", "Create a Bestselling Book Blueprint", "Bestseller
Outline", "The Blueprint Builder", "The Outline Architect", "Strategic Book Blueprint Developer".
