# Chapter writer (merged from 6 source prompts)

Write Chapter [NUMBER]: [CHAPTER TITLE] of my book titled [BOOK TITLE], focused on [TOPIC].
Follow this outline: [PASTE CHAPTER OUTLINE].

- Intended readership: [AUDIENCE]
- Narrative tone: [conversational / authoritative / story-driven]
- Target length: 2,000-3,000 words

The chapter must include:
- A compelling opening that immediately captures attention
- Three to four substantial sections organised with clear subheadings
- Real examples, engaging stories, scenarios, or mini case studies that ground the ideas
- Practical, actionable advice readers can apply immediately
- Short punchy paragraphs, strong active language
- A smooth bridge that sets up the next chapter
- A concise summary and 3 actionable steps the reader can act on immediately

Avoid AI clichés, repetition, filler, and generic phrasing.

## Introduction variant
Write a compelling introduction chapter that hooks the reader in the first 100 words and
previews the transformation this book delivers: [CONCEPT].

## Expander variant
Expand this outline point into a full 1,500-2,000 word chapter with examples and a clear
takeaway: [OUTLINE POINT].
