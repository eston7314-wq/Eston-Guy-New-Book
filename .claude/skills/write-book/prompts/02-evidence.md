# Evidence and authority integration

I am developing Chapter [X] on the topic of [SUBJECT]. Compile authoritative research and
supporting evidence, including:

- 3-5 peer-reviewed studies or academic papers with key findings summarised in accessible language
- 2-3 compelling statistics from reputable sources (industry reports, government data,
  established research institutions)
- 1-2 expert quotations or insights from recognised authorities in the field
- 1 relevant case study or real-world example that demonstrates the concept in action
- A brief synthesis paragraph explaining how this evidence collectively supports the chapter's
  central argument

Include proper citation format and a note on how each piece of evidence strengthens the
reader's trust and the book's credibility.

Source: "Evidence & Authority Integration Framework". Pipeline addition: nothing is cited
unless verified against a real source; unverifiable claims are flagged, never invented.
