# Title, subtitle, cover brief, back cover, description, TOC (merged from 6 source prompts)

Act as a professional book marketer and cover art director. My book is called [TITLE] and
targets [AUDIENCE]. Generate:

- 20 memorable titles, each with a compelling subtitle and a one-sentence value proposition,
  with 5 optimised for Amazon/KDP search
- Three cover concept directions (Minimal, Premium, Bold), each with colour palette,
  typography style, imagery description, layout, and the psychological trigger that makes
  someone pick it up; explain why each appeals to the target audience
- Back-cover copy under 100 words that sells the transformation and ends with a reason to buy now
- A clean chapter-by-chapter table of contents summary for the front matter

Sources merged: "The Book Cover Brief", "Create a Title, Subtitle & Cover Concept That Sells",
"Title & Cover Concept", "The Title Architect", "The Back-Cover Closer", "The TOC Builder".
